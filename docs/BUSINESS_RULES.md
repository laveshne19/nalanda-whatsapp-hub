# Business rules (set by the owner)

This file is the single place for rules the owner has given. Update it whenever a new rule is shared, and keep the bot's wording in line with it.

## Working days and hours

- We work Monday to Saturday, 11 AM to 7 PM (IST). Sunday is closed.
- Orders are dispatched on working days only.
- If a customer asks about an order over the weekend (or after 7 PM), it will be dispatched on the next working day. On Saturday evening or Sunday that means Monday.

## Replying to "is my order confirmed?"

1. Check that the order exists in Shopify (read only), matched to the sender's phone number.
2. If it exists, reply that it is confirmed and give the dispatch day using the rule above.
3. If it cannot be found, do not claim anything is confirmed. Ask for the order number, or pass the chat to the team.

## Scanning and replying

- The hub replies instantly to clear cases, with no AI cost.
- Chats the bot is not sure about are left for the team and listed in the daily report.
- Unread chats should be reviewed oldest first, about every 30 to 40 minutes.

## Tone

- Human, polite and professional. Short messages.
- Never use em dashes or en dashes in anything sent to a customer.
- The bot never cancels or refunds on its own. Those go to the team.

## Open questions for the owner

- Is the dispatch rule the same for every order, or only for orders placed after a cut-off time?
- Minimum order quantity for wholesale enquiries.
